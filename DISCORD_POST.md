#  Omarchy macOS Dock — The Authentic Frosted Glass Dock for Omarchy & Hyprland!

Hey Omarchy community! 👋 

I'm excited to share **macOS Dock (v1.1.0)** — an authentic, ultra-smooth, translucent frosted-glass macOS floating dock built natively for Omarchy Linux with Quickshell and Wayland.

---

### ✨ Key Features:
* 🍎 **Frosted Glass Floating Shelf** — Translucent backdrop with dynamic Omarchy theme blur, top specular rim reflection, and ambient depth shadow.
* 🎨 **Handcrafted Big Sur & Sonoma Vector Icons** — Smiling dual-tone Finder, Launchpad rocket, Mission Control workspaces, neon ECG Activity Monitor, and dynamic empty/full Trash bin.
* 🙈 **Intelligent Auto-Hide (Window Dodge)** — Rests cleanly on your desktop, and automatically glides down when application windows are open. Hover the bottom screen edge to reveal it instantly with zero jitter!
* 🌊 **Parabolic Magnification & Tooltips** — Fluid cubic zoom curves as your cursor sweeps across the dock, with high-contrast floating tooltip pills that never get cut off.
* 🪟 **Click-to-Minimize & Active Indicators** — Click an active app's dock icon to instantly minimize it to Hyprland's scratchpad; illuminated pills show focused apps.
* 🎛️ **Live Graphical Customizer** — Right-click the dock or click the settings gear to pin/unpin apps, reorder items, and live-scale from Compact (36px) to XL (72px) with persistent atomic saving.

---

### ⚡ Quick Install:
```bash
omarchy plugin add https://github.com/hari45wavin/omarchy-mac-dock.git --enable
```
